\section{Theoretical Context}

\subsection{Context on String Theory}
\noindent Based on \cite{Becker2007,Rickles_2014}.

String theory proposes that the fundamental elements of the
universe aren't adimentional particles but rather
one-dimentional strings of energy in a 10-dimentional space.

The mathematical framework of string theory shows us a way
of understanding for the quantum gravity. This feature gave it
the popularity it has today as a reliable search for the
theory of everything.

Current version of string theory has 5 mathematical homologeous
variants. This allows us to \say{change the window} with which
study the strings behaviour.

The most relevant challenges in string theory are given by
the swampland: \textit{since the string theory is a framework it has
the capacity to build a wide range of universes}.

Several conjectures and hypotheses about geometries where
strings lives, string types, branes interaction, etc. are
discussed currently, leading to a vast research field and to
the creation of the swampland project, aimed to find a way to
distinguish theories inconsistent with quantum gravity from
those that are consistent.


\subsection{Context on Metaheuristic Optimization}

Bioinspired algorithms are models inspired by the nature
allowing us to understand and or predict the behaviour of
complex systems \cite{Cotta_2017}. Those algorithms are not
necessarily strict-based on such phenomena but also inspired by
them \cite{Game2020}.

These algorithms are simple but their behaviour is complex,
non-linear and adaptable, permitting solving problems involving
similar complexity with ease \cite{Cotta_2017}.

However the effectiveness of any implementation is given by the
problem, the algorithm's components definition and the model
to optimize \cite{Cotta_2017, Yang_2016}.

Since there's a wide range of bio inspired algorithms aimed to
solve complex and non-linear problems, the first and most
important challenge is to find the one which fits better on the
optimization. Also, to find the correct description of the
phenomenon to optimize is crucial for a successful performance.
